1. A cinema or a showtime is missing

Listings are collected from each cinema's own website several times a day. A screening that was only just published can take a few hours to appear, and a cinema that changes its website can drop out until we adapt to it. If a venue you use has been missing for more than a day, email us its name and city and we will look at it.

2. A showtime or a booking link is wrong

Every showtime links straight to the cinema's own booking page — we never sell tickets and never take payment. The cinema's site is always the authority: if it disagrees with us, believe it, and please tell us so we can fix the listing.

3. A rating, poster or description looks wrong

Ratings come from IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic; posters and descriptions come from TMDB. Occasionally a film is matched to the wrong entry — most often a remake, or two films sharing a title. Send us the film and the city and we will correct the match.

4. Hiding films and cinemas

Tap the ✕ on a poster to hide a film you have already seen or do not care about. You can also switch off cinemas you never visit, so the listings show only the ones you would actually go to. Both lists can be reviewed and undone at any time from the app's toolbar.

5. Do I need an account?

No. Every listing, rating and film detail works without signing in. An account — Apple, Google or Facebook — does one thing: it syncs your hidden films and disabled cinemas across your devices. The app is free and carries no adverts.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account, and everything stored with it, from within the app. If you would rather we did it, email the address above from the address you signed up with and we will remove it. What we hold and why is set out in our privacy policy.
