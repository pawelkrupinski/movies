@(oauthProviders: Set[String], loginLabel: String)(implicit messages: play.api.i18n.Messages) @* The auth slot: the pill that opens the login modal, on the repertoire navbar, the film page and the browse page — which is why it lives here rather than three times over. `loginLabel` is the only thing that differs: the repertoire navbar competes for width with the day pills and the search box, so it says the short form. THE AVATAR IS NOT RENDERED HERE ANY MORE, and that is the whole point. A page that draws somebody's avatar, name and account menu is a page that differs per visitor, and a response that differs per visitor can never be handed to a shared cache — see `MovieController.SharedMaxAgeSeconds`, which spells out that only byte-identical responses may carry `s-maxage`. So the server renders the SIGNED-OUT slot for everyone and `shared.js` asks `/api/me` who is looking, building the avatar menu into this slot after first paint (`hydrateAuth`). The pill is rendered even for a visitor who turns out to be signed in, hidden by that hydration rather than absent — signing out is a redirect chain across two domains, and for as long as it runs the visitor is still looking at this page, so shared.js swaps the two rather than leaving a hole where the way back in should be. Hidden via `style.display` rather than the `hidden` attribute, because `.nav-tab-login` sets a `display` of its own and would win over the attribute; hiding it inline means showing it again is `display = ''`, which hands the slot back to the stylesheet — including the mobile rule that keeps the pill out of the navbar entirely.
