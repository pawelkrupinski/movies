This policy explains what personal data @models.Country.fromEnv.brandName (cinema listings, available on the web and as an Android and iOS app) processes, for what purpose and on what basis, and what rights you have in relation to that processing.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Paweł Krupiński, the author of the app. For any question about privacy or data protection, write to @controllers.LegalContact.Email.

2. What data we process

Using the app without signing in requires no personal data at all. Once you sign in, we process:

  • Sign-in data from Facebook, Google or Apple — your user identifier at that provider, your email address (if you share it), your first and last name as a display name, and the URL of your profile picture.
  • Account settings — your preferences in the app, namely the list of hidden films and disabled cinemas, synchronised across your devices.
  • Strictly necessary cookies — a signed session cookie that keeps you signed in. We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

The mobile apps may ask for access to your location in order to suggest the nearest city. Your location is used on the device only — it is never sent to us and never stored.

Listings, ratings and film descriptions come from public third-party sources (TMDB, Filmweb, IMDb and others) and are not our users' personal data.

3. Purpose and legal basis

  • Authenticating you and running your account, and synchronising your settings across devices — necessary to provide the service you asked for: Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR / GDPR.
  • Keeping the app secure and working correctly — our legitimate interest: Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR / GDPR.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with third parties for marketing. It is processed only by the providers that technically make the app work, acting on our instructions:

  • Sign-in providers — Meta (Facebook), Google and Apple, who supply the sign-in data, under their own privacy policies.
  • Infrastructure providers — the app hosting and the database your account is stored in.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as you use the app. When the account is deleted, everything associated with it is permanently erased (see section 7).

6. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it,
  • have inaccurate data corrected,
  • have your data erased (the "right to be forgotten"),
  • restrict processing,
  • data portability,
  • object to processing,
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the United Kingdom, or your national authority in the EU.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time from inside the app — choose the delete-account option in your account settings. That permanently erases your profile and your saved preferences. You can also ask us to delete your data by writing to @controllers.LegalContact.Email.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app develops. The current version is always available at this address, and the date it was last updated appears at the top of the page.
