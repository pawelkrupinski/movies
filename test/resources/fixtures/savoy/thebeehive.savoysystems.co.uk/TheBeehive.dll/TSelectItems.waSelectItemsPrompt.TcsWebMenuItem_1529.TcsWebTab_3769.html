A Very Dickensian Murder (18)

A Very Dickensian Murder presented by the Candlelight Theatre Company
Running Time: 2 & 1/2 Hours 
Tickets £39.50. All tickets include a 2 course dinner and a £2 booking fee

It is 1893 and the Widow Snazbatten doesn't have a lot of money but she has saved a few pounds from her yearly wage to throw a party for all her friends at her favourite place - Belcher Towers. She is looking forward to seeing everyone from William Marley, her close friend Prudence and her new friend, a young writer by the name of Charles Dickens. Of course, no party can go without a hitch and what will happen if they disturb the resident miser Cyrus Belcher?

 Join Candlelight Murder Mystery Company this Christmas and see if all ends up merry and bright

Courtroom Live (16)

Courtroom Live
Running Time: Up to 3 Hours. (Including Interval)
Suitable for aged 16+
Tickets: General Admission £25. All tickets include a £2 booking fee.
Courtroom Live plunges you straight into the high-stakes case of Jason Tyler, a well respected vet suddenly accused of killing his wife. Based on a true case, this gripping interactive experience blurs the line between fiction and reality from the moment you take your seat.
 You are no longer a spectator, you become part of the trial itself. Throughout the evening you will listen to powerful opening statements, hear raw and conflicting testimony from key witnesses and examine the evidence laid out before you. Nothing is off-limits, you can question the witnesses directly, challenge their stories and even cross-examine Jason Tyler himself as he sits in the dock fighting for his freedom.
 Every answer could shift your opinion. Every piece of evidence might hide a darker truth. Motives, alibis, secrets and lies will all come under the spotlight as the case unfolds in real time around you. 
When the final arguments have been heard, the decision rests entirely with you. Guilty or not guilty, Jason Tyler’s fate is in your hands.
 This fully interactive live courtroom experience is brought to you by Cheeky Reaper where the drama is real, the tension is electric, and the verdict is yours alone.
